Privacy Policy — WordRota

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what WordRota ("the app", "the game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the iOS app published by LDH ("we", "us").

WordRota has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no in-app purchases. You never type personal information into it.

Short version

  • WordRota collects no data at all and never connects to the internet.
  • Your game progress and your language choice stay on your device.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

What stays on your device

Your current level in each word language (English, French, Spanish) and the language you picked are stored locally on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them permanently — we hold no backup and cannot restore them.

The word lists and levels are built into the app. Checking your words happens entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app's App Store listing ("App Privacy") states "Data Not Collected". The app contains no third-party SDK and makes no network request.

Crash and diagnostic data

If the app crashes, Apple may share technical details about the failure with us if you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your iPhone settings — device model, OS version, and where in the code it happened. This is handled by Apple, helps us fix the bug, and contains nothing about you.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not ask for, store or transmit your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not access your photos, camera, microphone, location or health data.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent any data.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you the right to know what is collected and to request its deletion. Since WordRota collects no personal data, we hold nothing about you; you can still write to us with any question.

Contact: leducholding@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Children

WordRota is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the app handles data, we will update this page first, and the App Store listing will say what that version collects.

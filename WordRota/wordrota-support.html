WordRota — Support

An iOS word puzzle game published by LDH.

Need help?

Write to leducholding@gmail.com. We read every message and answer within a few days. Tell us your device model, iOS version and the level number if you are reporting a bug — it helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I play?

Words are stacked on top of each other. The letters inside the outlined columns are mixed up. Drag a column up or down: its letters roll round, and the one leaving the top comes back at the bottom. Line up the right letters so every row spells a word.

Why did a word turn green?

It is correct. A word found locks in place, and the columns crossing it lock too, so you cannot undo it by mistake.

What is the picture above the grid?

It shows one of the words to find. Spot which row it is, and you have a head start.

I am stuck on a level.

Tap Hint: a small badge shows which letter belongs in a column cell.

How do I earn 3 stars?

Finish without a hint and in few moves (about two moves per column).

Can I play in another language?

Yes. Tap the flag on the home screen to switch the words between English, French and Spanish. Each language has its own levels and its own progress.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. WordRota is fully playable offline.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app erases it; there is no account and no cloud save.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

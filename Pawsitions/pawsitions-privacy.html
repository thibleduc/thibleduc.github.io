Privacy Policy — Pawsitions

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what Pawsitions ("the app", "the game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Android and iOS (iPhone) apps published by LDH ("we", "us").

Pawsitions is a free single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases, and you never type personal information into it. It is funded by ads shown by Google AdMob (on Android).

Short version

  • Your game data (the level you reached) stays on your device.
  • The app sends anonymous gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics, to see which levels are too hard and to improve the game.
  • The app tells Meta (Facebook) that it was installed and opened, when you finish the tutorial and when you finish a level, so we can tell which of our Facebook and Instagram adverts bring in new players. On iPhone, your advertising identifier is shared with Meta only if you allow tracking; on Android, it is never shared with Meta.
  • On Android, the game shows ads between levels and offers optional video ads in exchange for a hint. These ads come from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and IP address to choose ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, ads are personalised only if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time with the shield button at the top left of the screen.
  • We never sell your data, and we cannot identify you personally from what we collect.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the level you reached and whether you finished the tutorial) is stored locally on your phone. It is not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it permanently — we hold no backup and cannot restore it.

What the app collects, and why

The app's Google Play listing ("Data safety") and App Store listing ("App Privacy") always state what the version you download actually collects.

1. Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

As you play, the app sends anonymous events to GameAnalytics, our analytics provider: when you start the tutorial, finish each of its steps and complete it; when you start and finish a level; and when you restart a level. Each event contains:

  • what happened, as described above;
  • your device model, operating system version, app version and country;
  • how long the play session lasted;
  • a random identifier generated on install, which is not linked to your name, your email or your Google or Apple account;
  • on iPhone, the identifier iOS gives to all apps from the same publisher on your device (IDFV) and — only if you allow tracking (see below) — your advertising identifier (IDFA); on Android, your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID), unless you deleted it in your phone's settings.

Why: to see which levels players find too hard or too easy, and to improve the game. Nothing else.

Legal basis (GDPR): our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the game.

GameAnalytics acts as our data processor. Their privacy policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

2. Advertising measurement (Meta / Facebook)

We advertise Pawsitions on Facebook and Instagram. The app includes Meta's software kit (the Facebook SDK) so we can tell which of those adverts bring in players. It tells Meta that the app was installed and opened, when you finish the tutorial and which level you finished, along with basic technical information such as your device model, operating system version and IP address.

  • On Android, the app tells Meta's kit not to collect your advertising identifier, so it is never shared with Meta. Meta may still learn which advert led to an install through the referral information Google Play provides when the app is first opened.
  • On iPhone, the first time you open the app, iOS asks whether you allow tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you decline, the app does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA) and Meta is told that you did not allow tracking. If you allow it, the app also shares your advertising identifier with Meta, so Meta can match your install to the advert you saw.

Why: so we can tell which of our adverts actually bring in players, and stop paying for the ones that do not.

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent, given through the iOS prompt, for the advertising identifier on iPhone; our legitimate interest in measuring our advertising for the install, open, tutorial and level events.

You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID). Declining changes nothing in the game.

Meta acts as an independent data controller for this data. Their privacy policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

3. Ads in the game (Google AdMob, Android)

The Android version shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then between two levels (never in your first two minutes of play, and at most one every 90 seconds), and an optional video you can choose to watch to get a hint. To choose, show and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, Google receives:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and an app-set identifier;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) is derived;
  • technical information such as your device model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with the ads (views, taps, whether a video was watched to the end).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app first shows Google's consent message (certified consent management platform). If you do not consent, you still get ads, but they are not personalised. To change your choice later, tap the shield button at the top left of the game screen.

Elsewhere, you can turn off personalised ads by deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).

Why: the ads pay for the game, which is free.

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent for personalised ads and for storing or accessing information on your device; our legitimate interest for showing non-personalised ads and preventing fraud.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. How Google uses it: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites — Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Crash and diagnostic data

If the app crashes, Google Play (on Android, if you share usage and diagnostics with Google) or Apple (on iPhone, if you have chosen to share analytics with developers) may share technical details about the failure with us — device model, OS version, and where in the code it happened. This helps us fix it. It contains nothing about you.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell anything inside the game.
  • We do not ask for, store or transmit your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not access your photos, camera, microphone, location or health data.
  • We do not run any server of our own, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not build advertising profiles about you ourselves.

How long data is kept

Anonymous gameplay statistics are retained by GameAnalytics for as long as they remain useful for improving the game, and no longer than 24 months. Advertising measurement data is retained by Meta according to their own policy.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you the right to know what is collected and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information as the CCPA defines it.

Because our analytics data is anonymous and carries no identifier that we can trace back to you, we may need you to provide the random install identifier before we can act on a request. Contact us and we will explain how to find it.

To exercise any of these rights, or to withdraw consent, write to leducholding@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Pawsitions is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page first. Significant changes will be announced in the app's release notes.

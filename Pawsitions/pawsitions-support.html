Pawsitions — Support

A cozy corgi logic puzzle for Android and iPhone, published by LDH.

Need help?

Write to leducholding@gmail.com. We read every message and answer within a few days. Tell us your phone model and its Android or iOS version if you are reporting a bug — it helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I play?

Drag every piece onto the board until it is full. Each piece carries one corgi, and there must be exactly one corgi in every row and every column. Pieces never rotate. Pinned pieces are already in the right place.

I am stuck on a level.

Every level has exactly one solution, and you can always find the next piece by logic — no guessing needed. Darker squares are in a row or column that already has a corgi: no other corgi can go there. Look for a piece that only fits in one place, or a row that only one corgi can reach. You can tap the restart button at any time.

Why does a corgi look worried?

Two corgis are in the same row or column. Drag one of the pieces back to the tray or somewhere else.

How many levels are there?

There is no end: after the first 100 levels, new puzzles are created for you endlessly, from 6×6 to 9×9.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Pawsitions is fully playable offline. When you are online, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics and advertising measurement events, as explained in the privacy policy.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device only. There is no account and no cloud save: deleting the app erases it, and we cannot restore it.

Are there ads or purchases?

Nothing to buy. On Android, the free game shows an ad now and then between two levels (never in your first two minutes of play), and you can watch an optional video to get a hint. Your first hint is free.

How do hints work?

Tap the light bulb: any misplaced piece goes back to the tray, and the next piece you could find by logic flies to its spot.

How do I change my ad consent?

In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, tap the shield button at the top left of the game screen.

Sponge Slide — Support

An iOS puzzle game published by LDH.

Need help?

Write to leducholding@gmail.com. We read every message and answer within a few days. Tell us your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug — it helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I play?

Swipe up, down, left or right. The sponge slides until it hits a wall and scrubs every tile it crosses. Clean every tile to finish the level.

I am stuck on a level.

Tap Hint: an arrow shows the next good swipe. Tap Restart to start the level again.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Sponge Slide is fully playable offline.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app erases it; there is no account and no cloud save.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

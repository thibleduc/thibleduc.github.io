Slider Cubes! — Support

An iOS puzzle game published by LDH.

Need help?

Write to leducholding@gmail.com. We read every message and answer within a few days. Tell us your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug — it helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I play?

Swipe up, down, left or right. Your cube slides in that direction until it hits a wall, an edge or another cube. Your goal is to land each cube on the portal of its colour.

The cube won't stop where I want.

That's the puzzle! A cube never stops mid-track — it only stops when something blocks it. Use the walls and the other cubes to park each one exactly on its portal.

There are several cubes — how does that work?

All cubes move at the same time, in the same direction, with a single swipe. Once a cube is parked on a portal it locks in place and becomes a wall for the others. Cover every portal to win.

What are the coins for?

Collect coins while you play, then spend them in the shop (the bag icon, top-left) to unlock cube skins. Every tenth level is a bonus level filled with coins.

How do I get 3 stars?

Finish a level in as few moves as possible. Fewer moves means more stars.

I made a mistake.

Use Undo to take back your last move, or Restart to begin the level again.

Does the game need an internet connection?

The puzzles are fully playable offline. An internet connection is only used to show ads.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app erases it; there is no account and no cloud save.

Are there ads or purchases?

There are occasional ads between levels. There are no real-money purchases — cube skins are bought with coins you earn by playing.

Stacked Cubes — Support

An iOS puzzle game published by LDH.

Need help?

Write to leducholding@gmail.com. We read every message and answer within a few days. Tell us your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug — it helps a lot.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Stacked Cubes is fully playable offline.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app erases it; there is no account and no cloud save.

How do I rotate a piece?

Tap the piece before dragging it onto the platform.

How do I change my tracking choice?

Open the iOS Settings app, then Privacy & Security → Tracking, and switch Stacked Cubes on or off. The game works exactly the same either way.

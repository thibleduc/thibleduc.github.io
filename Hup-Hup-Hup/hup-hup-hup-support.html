Hup Hup Hup — Support

An iOS arcade game published by LDH.

Need help?

Write to leducholding@gmail.com. We read every message and answer within a few days. Tell us your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug — it helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I play?

Your balloon rises on its own. Drag left or right to spin the tower and line up a gap above the balloon so it can float through. Reach the checkered finish ring to clear the level.

My balloon keeps bumping into a floor.

Spin the tower until an opening is right above the balloon. Each time it bumps, the balloon bounces back down for a moment: use it to swing the next gap into place.

What does the double arrow do?

Float through it to grab a boost: your balloon blasts straight through the next five floors.

Who are the other balloons?

They are computer-controlled rivals that race you to the top. There is no online play and no other real players.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Hup Hup Hup is fully playable offline.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app erases it; there is no account and no cloud save.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

Privacy Policy — MyPhoto Guesser

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what MyPhoto Guesser ("the app", "the game") does with your data. It covers the iOS app published by LDH ("we", "us").

MyPhoto Guesser has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

Short version

  • The app plays with your own photos, on your iPhone only. No photo is ever uploaded.
  • We collect no data at all. The app's App Store listing states "Data Not Collected".
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sold.

What stays on your device

  • Your photo library. With your permission, the app reads the photos that have a GPS location, shows them to you and compares your guess with their location. If you give limited access, only the photos you selected are used. Photos and their locations are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Photo analysis. To only play photos showing people, landscapes or food, the app analyses them with Apple's on-device Vision framework. The result (play / skip) is cached on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Your settings: number of rounds and time per round.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. We hold no copy and cannot restore it.

Maps and place names

Maps are displayed with Apple Maps (MapKit). After each round, the app asks Apple for the name of the place where the photo was taken (for example "Paris, France"): the photo's coordinates are sent to Apple for that purpose only, without the photo. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive nothing.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not upload your photos or their locations.
  • We do not show ads and do not use any advertising or analytics SDK.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent any data.

Crash and diagnostic data

If the app crashes, Apple may share technical details about the failure with us if you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your iPhone settings — device model, OS version, and where in the code it happened. This is handled by Apple and contains nothing about you.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you the right to know what is collected and to request its deletion. Since MyPhoto Guesser collects no personal data, we hold nothing about you; you can still write to us with any question.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data online, this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new date.

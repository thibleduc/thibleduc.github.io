These terms apply to MyPhoto Guesser ("the app"), published by LDH. By creating or joining a game with friends, you accept them.

Games with friends

Games with friends let you share photos and videos with the people who have the game code, the QR code or the invitation link. You are responsible for what you share.

What you may share

  • Only photos and videos that you took, or that you have the right to share.
  • Only pictures of people who agree to appear in them.
  • Never content that is offensive, violent, sexual, hateful, harassing, discriminatory or illegal, or that reveals someone's private information.

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and its author may be excluded from games with friends.

Reporting and blocking

  • Open the game and tap Report this game to report inappropriate photos, private information, spam or abuse. You can also write to us with the game code.
  • In the Players list of a game, tap Block to hide a player's photos and scores on your iPhone, in every game. You can block and report at the same time.
  • We review every report within 24 hours, remove the content concerned and, where needed, exclude the user who shared it.

Your content

You keep all rights to your photos and videos. By adding them to a game, you allow the other players of that game to see them, only within the app. The creator of a game can delete it at any time, with all its photos.

The app is provided as is. Games with friends rely on Apple's iCloud and may be temporarily unavailable. We may change these terms; the date above shows the latest version.

MyPhoto Guesser — Support

An iOS photo geography game published by LDH.

Need help?

Write to leducholding@gmail.com. We read every message and answer within a few days. Tell us your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug — it helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I play?

The app shows you one of your own geotagged photos. Look at it, tap Place your pin, drop a pin on the world map where you think it was taken, then confirm. The closer you are, the more points you score (up to 5,000 per round).

Why are some of my photos never shown?

Only photos with a precise GPS location are used, and only those showing people, a landscape or food (screenshots and documents are skipped). Photos taken with location services turned off, or received through some messaging apps that remove the location, cannot be played.

I have no geotagged photos.

Tap Play with demo photos to play with famous places built into the app.

I don't like this photo.

Tap Change photo under the photo to get another one.

Why do I have to guess the year?

When the photo has a date, you score points for the place and for the year it was taken: up to 5,000 each. Demo photos have no date, so only the place counts.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Create a game, pick the dates of a trip and the photos you want to play with. The app then shows a QR code and a link: your friends scan the code with their iPhone camera or open the link, and the game opens in their app. Everyone plays whenever they want and appears on the same leaderboard. Playing with friends requires being signed in to iCloud.

Who can see the photos of my game?

Anyone with the game code, the QR code or the link. Only the pictures are uploaded, without their metadata; their place and date are revealed after each answer. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete a game?

Open it from My games and tap Delete this game. Its photos are removed for everyone. Only the person who created the game can delete it.

How do I report a game?

Open the game and tap Report this game, or write to us with its code. We review every report and remove content that breaks the rules.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

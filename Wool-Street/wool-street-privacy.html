Privacy Policy — Wool Street

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what Wool Street ("the app", "the game", listed on the App Store and Google Play as "Wool Street: Sheep Tycoon") collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the iOS (iPhone) and Android apps published by LDH ("we", "us").

Wool Street is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and you never type personal information into it. On Android, the game is free: it shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a starter pack and "No ads"); see sections 4 and 5. On iPhone, the game currently shows no ads and offers no purchases: its coin shop only accepts coins you earn by playing.

Short version

  • Your game data stays on your device.
  • The app sends anonymous gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics, to see where players get stuck and to balance the game.
  • The app tells Meta (Facebook) that it was installed and opened, and when you finish the tutorial, so we can tell which of our Facebook and Instagram adverts bring in new players. On iPhone, your advertising identifier is shared with Meta only if you allow tracking; on Android, the app never reads it.
  • On Android, ads are shown by Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it at any time with "Privacy choices" at the bottom of the coin shop.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your payment details.
  • We never sell your data, and we cannot identify you personally from what we collect.

Animals, customers and staff

The sheep, llamas, customers, shepherds and other characters in the game are computer-controlled and simulated on your device. There is no online play: nothing about you or your farm is sent to other players, and no other player's data is shown to you.

What stays on your device

Your game data (your progress, your settings and your language choice) is stored locally on your phone. It is not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it permanently — we hold no backup and cannot restore it.

What the app collects, and why

The app's App Store listing ("App Privacy") and its Google Play listing ("Data safety") always state what the version you download actually collects.

1. Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

As you play, the app sends anonymous events to GameAnalytics, our analytics provider: when you start the tutorial, finish each of its steps and complete it; when you reach a goal; when you buy something on the farm with in-game money (what you bought and its in-game price); when you buy something in the coin shop; when the wolf eats one of your animals; and, on Android, when you watch an ad (and the advertising revenue Google reports for it), when you open the purchase window or buy something (the product, its price and currency), and the fact that you answered the consent form (not what you chose). Each event contains:

  • what happened, as described above;
  • which version of the game you have (see section 6);
  • your device model, operating system version, app version and country;
  • how long the play session lasted;
  • a random identifier generated on install, which is not linked to your name, your email or your Apple or Google account;
  • the identifier iOS gives to all apps from the same publisher on your device (IDFV) and — only if you allow tracking (see below) — your advertising identifier (IDFA). GameAnalytics uses them to count players for us, not to show you ads. On Android, the app removes access to the advertising identifier (Android advertising ID) entirely, so it is never collected.

Why: to see where players get stuck, and to balance prices and difficulty. Nothing else.

Legal basis (GDPR): our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the game.

GameAnalytics acts as our data processor. Their privacy policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

2. Advertising measurement (Meta / Facebook)

We advertise Wool Street on Facebook and Instagram. The app includes Meta's software kit (the Facebook SDK) so we can tell which of those adverts bring in players. It tells Meta that the app was installed and opened, and when you finish the tutorial, along with basic technical information such as your device model, operating system version and IP address.

On iPhone, the first time you open the app, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. This is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and our message in it reads: "This only helps us see which of our ads brought you to Wool Street, so we can find more players like you."

  • If you decline (or ignore it), the app does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA) and Meta is told that you did not allow tracking, so nothing is linked to you across other companies' apps and websites. We only see overall counts in Meta's reports.
  • If you allow it, the app also shares your advertising identifier with Meta, so Meta can match your install to the advert you saw.
  • On Android, there is no such prompt and the app never reads your advertising identifier, so it is never shared with Meta. Meta may still learn which advert led to an install through the referral information Google Play provides when the app is first opened.

Why: so we can tell which of our adverts actually bring in players, and stop paying for the ones that do not.

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent, given through the iOS prompt, for the advertising identifier on iPhone; our legitimate interest in measuring our advertising for the install, open and tutorial events.

You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone. On Android, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads), although the app does not use it. Declining changes nothing in the game: every feature works exactly the same either way.

Meta acts as an independent data controller for this data. Their privacy policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

3. Crash and diagnostic data

If the app crashes, Apple (on iPhone, if you have chosen to share analytics with developers) or Google Play (on Android, if you share usage and diagnostics with Google) may share technical details about the failure with us — device model, OS version, and where in the code it happened. This helps us fix it. It contains nothing about you.

4. Ads (Google AdMob, Android only)

On Android, the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which helps keep it free:

  • videos you choose to watch in exchange for a reward (for example coins, a free boost or doubled earnings) — you can always say no;
  • depending on the version of the game you have (see section 6), a full-screen ad from time to time, at most once a minute and never during the tutorial. Buying "No ads" removes these full-screen ads; the optional reward videos stay available.

To request and show ads, Google's software kit (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) collects information such as your IP address (and the approximate location it reveals), your device model, operating system and language, an app-specific identifier provided by Google Play services (App Set ID), and how you interact with the ads (views, clicks), along with diagnostic data. The Android app removes access to your advertising identifier (Android advertising ID), so it is never collected.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the game Google's consent form (Google's certified consent management platform) asks whether Google and its advertising partners may use your data for personalised ads and store or access information on your device. You can accept, refuse ("Do not consent") or choose purpose by purpose ("Manage options"). No ad is requested before you answer. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which do not use your data for profiling. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: open the coin shop in the game and tap "Privacy choices" at the bottom.

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent, for personalised ads and for storing or accessing information on your device; our legitimate interest, for non-personalised ads and for preventing fraud.

Google acts as an independent data controller for this data. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites — Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. In-app purchases (Google Play, Android only)

On Android, you can buy coin packs, a starter pack and "No ads" with real money. These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play: we never see or store your name, your payment card or your billing details. Google Play gives the app the product you bought and an order number. The app keeps the order number on your device so that a purchase is never delivered twice, and asks Google Play for your past one-time purchases (the starter pack and "No ads") so that they come back if you reinstall the game. The product, its price and its currency are also sent to GameAnalytics (section 1) so we can see which offers players like.

Legal basis (GDPR): the performance of the purchase contract you enter into.

Google Play's terms and refund policy apply to every purchase. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Two versions of the game (Android)

To find out which version players enjoy more, each new Android player is randomly given one of two versions of the game the first time they open it: one with full-screen ads from time to time, and one with optional reward videos only. Both have the same content; "No ads" is only offered in the version with full-screen ads. Which version you have is stored on your device and sent with the gameplay statistics (section 1), so we can compare the two versions as a whole. It is never used to identify you. Players who had started the game before this test keep a version with no ads and no purchases.

Legal basis (GDPR): our legitimate interest in improving the game.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not show ads on iPhone, and on Android we do not show ads during the tutorial.
  • We do not see, store or transmit your payment details: Google Play handles every purchase.
  • We do not ask for, store or transmit your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not access your photos, camera, microphone, location or health data.
  • We do not run any server of our own, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not build advertising profiles about you ourselves.

How long data is kept

Anonymous gameplay statistics are retained by GameAnalytics for as long as they remain useful for improving the game, and no longer than 24 months. Advertising measurement data is retained by Meta, and ad and purchase data by Google, according to their own policies. Order numbers kept on your device are deleted with the app.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you the right to know what is collected and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information as the CCPA defines it.

Because our analytics data is anonymous and carries no identifier that we can trace back to you, we may need you to provide the random install identifier before we can act on a request. Contact us and we will explain how to find it.

You can withdraw your consent for ads at any time in the game (coin shop → "Privacy choices"). To exercise any of these rights, write to leducholding@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Wool Street is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page first. Significant changes will be announced in the app's release notes.

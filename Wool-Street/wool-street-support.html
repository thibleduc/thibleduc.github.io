Wool Street — Support

An iOS idle farming game published by LDH.

Need help?

Write to leducholding@gmail.com. We read every message and answer within a few days. Tell us your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug — it helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I play?

Slide your finger anywhere on the screen to walk. Walk up to a sheep to shear it, carry the wool to the stall, then stand on the pile of banknotes to collect what your customers paid. Stop on a square to buy what it shows: more sheep, a bigger pen, staff, machines and, later, a workshop that turns wool into clothes.

My wool has stopped growing.

Your animals need hay and water. When a gauge turns red, stand on the HAY or WATER circle next to the pen until the trough is full again. Broken fences, hurt animals and births also need you: follow the instruction at the bottom of the screen.

A wolf keeps eating my animals.

Hire the sheepdog: it chases the wolf away for good and brings back any animal that runs off.

What are the coins for?

You earn coins by completing tutorial steps and goals. Tap the coin counter at the top left to open the shop and spend them on boosts, outfits for your shepherd and special sheep. Coins cannot be bought with real money.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Wool Street is fully playable offline. When you are online, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics and advertising measurement events, as explained in the privacy policy.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device only. There is no account and no cloud save: deleting the app erases it, and we cannot restore it.

How do I start over?

Delete the app and install it again from the App Store. This erases everything stored on your device and starts a brand-new farm.

The game asked me to allow tracking. What does it change?

Nothing in the game. Allowing it only lets Meta link your install to the Facebook or Instagram advert that brought you here. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. There are no ads in the game and nothing to buy with real money.

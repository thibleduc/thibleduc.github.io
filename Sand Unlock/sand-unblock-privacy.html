Privacy Policy — Sand Unblock

Last updated: 18 September 2026 — applies to version 1.0

This policy explains what Sand Unblock ("the app", "the game") does with your data. It covers the iOS app published by LDH ("we", "us").

Short version

  • Sand Unblock collects nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • The game makes no network connection at all. It works fully offline.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress stays on your device and never leaves it.

What stays on your device

Your current level, your coin total and the furthest level you have finished are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them permanently — we hold no backup and cannot restore them.

What we collect

Nothing. Version 1.0 contains no analytics tool, no advertising tool and no third-party software of any kind. It does not read your advertising identifier, does not ask for permission to track you, and does not access your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, location or health data.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to sell, share, correct or delete.

Future versions

A later update may add two things, and this page will be updated before it ships:

  • anonymous gameplay statistics (which levels are started and finished), so we can balance the difficulty;
  • advertising measurement, only if you allow it through Apple's tracking-permission prompt, so we can tell which of our adverts bring in new players.

Neither is present in the current version. If they are added, the App Store privacy label and this policy will say so, and declining will never change anything in the game.

Children

Sand Unblock is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

The GDPR (European Union, United Kingdom) and the CCPA (California) give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none. If you have any question about this policy, write to leducholding@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated first and the change will be announced in the app's release notes.
